What you can do

JalSip is a simple water reminder for everyday hydration. Unlock chests as you hit intake milestones. Drink logs stay on the device.

Log each glass

See today’s goal

No account needed

Use the app without sign-up. History stays on the phone.

How it works

JalSip stores your log, goal and history on the device. Notification permission is only used for drink reminders. If you deny it, you can still log water. Chests are an in-app motivation layer. Coins stay local. This app does not send you money and does not open a bank or wallet account.

The app may show rewarded video and interstitial ads. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings. JalSip is built for hydration, not for banking.
